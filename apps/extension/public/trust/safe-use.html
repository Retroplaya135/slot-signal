Slot Signal Trust Center

Safe Use Terms

The assistant may collect user-entered visible slot signals, show context, send browser or digest alerts, and prepare a visible handoff. The user must complete any appointment-changing decision in the official browser page.

Required Boundaries

  • No live portal requests.
  • No unattended booking.
  • No live email, Telegram, or Slack provider delivery in this MVP.
  • No hidden submit, background appointment change, or credential collection.
  • No bypass, evasion, or browser identity manipulation patterns.
