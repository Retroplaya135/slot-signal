Home / Trust

Trust starts with a visible boundary.

This beta is built around community signals, local extension access, and manual booking decisions. It is not a credential-sharing service and not an automatic booking agent.

Direct answer

What is safe to share? Visible appointment metadata that you choose to report. Do not share portal passwords, one-time codes, passport scans, DS-160 identifiers, or payment data.

Trust controls

The product is intentionally limited until backend entitlement, support, and provider delivery are fully reviewed.

Manual booking

Alerts do not make appointment changes. You review official pages yourself.

Local access key

The extension stores its access key in the browser profile, not in public pages.

Provenance labels

Signals carry freshness, source lane, confidence, and conflict context.

Glossary

Plain terms used across the public site and extension.

Signal

A visible appointment-related report or public-context clue that may be relevant to a watch filter.

Provenance

The source, freshness, and confidence metadata shown before a signal is trusted.

Access code

The current beta delivery mechanism. It is not a payment checkout and not a portal credential.
